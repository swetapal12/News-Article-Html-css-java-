1️⃣ What is Network Security?

Network security refers to policies and practices to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or modification of network resources. It ensures data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

2️⃣ Types of Network Security

  • Firewalls: Monitor and control network traffic.
  • Antivirus Software: Detects and removes malicious software.
  • Encryption: Protects sensitive information during transmission.
  • VPN: Creates secure connections over the internet.

3️⃣ Importance of Network Security

It prevents cyberattacks, protects identity and data, and ensures smooth network operations for individuals and businesses.
